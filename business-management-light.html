See It In Action

A Look Inside Business Management Pro

Real screens from the workspace — from the home screen and POS billing to sales, purchases, partners and accounts.

One Home Screen for Every Module

Every part of the business sits on one home screen: Dashboard, POS, Inventory, Sales, Purchase, Partners, Accounts, Reports and Settings. A branch selector at the top lets you switch between locations without leaving the workspace.

Home POS Inventory Sales Accounts

Fast POS Billing

Ring up a sale from one clean screen. Tap a product card to add it to the cart, see available stock on every item, apply 15% VAT with a single toggle, and check out with the subtotal, tax and total updated as you go.

Products Cart 15% VAT Checkout

Live Business Dashboard

Track the health of the business at a glance: total products, total inventory value, low-stock items, branches and warehouses. The inventory movement chart compares stock in, stock out and transfers across 7, 30, 90 days or a year, with quick actions and low-stock alerts beside it.

KPIs Movement Charts Low-Stock Alerts Quick Actions

Inventory Movement Control

Follow every receipt, delivery, transfer and return as it happens. The last 30 days are summarised at the top, and the movements table shows each IN or OUT entry with its items, total quantity and amount, so stock levels always match what moved.

Receipts Deliveries Transfers Returns

Sales Order Management

Create quotations and sales orders, then follow each one from draft to confirmed. Filter by status, search by customer or reference, and see total revenue and pending orders at a glance.

Orders Order Status Search Revenue

Purchase Order Control

Manage vendor orders from one workflow: approve, track and receive. See total orders, confirmed orders, total spending and pending approvals at the top, then filter the list by draft, sent or confirmed status.

Orders Vendors Spend Receive

Partner & Customer Management

Keep customers, vendors and contacts in one searchable list. Each partner shows its contact details and a type badge — Customer, Vendor or Both — and the totals at the top show how your network is split.

Customers Vendors Contacts Search

Accounts & Financial Reports

Keep the books in the same system as sales and purchases. Track total assets, liabilities and net equity, and see profit for the month. Jump to a new journal entry, the chart of accounts, trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet or general ledger, and review recent activity in one list.

Assets Ledger Trial Balance Profit & Loss
